PHATT Tech builds AI tooling, custom APIs, and data warehouses. Self-hosted where it matters.

A small Ohio shop that ships software the way we’d want it shipped to us: documented, reproducible, runnable by the buyer after handoff.

What we build

AI tooling & agent systems

Self-hosted, bring-your-own-model, wired into your existing stack. Agents that do work — with verification gates, runbooks, and a kill switch — not chatbots. Built around an org chart with defined owners, not magic prompts.

Custom APIs & integrations

Glue between systems that were never meant to talk. Webhook receivers, scheduled sync workers, MCP servers, third-party connectors. Versioned, observable, and documented well enough to hand off.

Data warehouses & reporting

From scattered spreadsheets and vendor exports to one queryable source of truth. Schema you can hand to a new hire and have them writing reports by Friday. Refresh jobs you can read.

Custom broadcast appliances

Bespoke retro cable-channel builds: scheduled programming, era-correct commercials and idents, CRT output, phone-based program director, seasonal packages, disaster-recovery image. Delivered as an appliance with a runbook.

How we work

  • Nothing ships without real verification: screenshots, transcripts, smoke tests, the receipts.
  • You get the runbook and the repo. We hand off, you own it. No black boxes.
  • We use our own tooling in production before we sell it. If we won’t run it, we won’t ship it.
